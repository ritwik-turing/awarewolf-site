what i said in the reel

can i tell you something?

there was a ten-year-old version of you who loved playing… anything. everything. he or she ran outside for no reason. no goals. no plan. whatever was there… that was the game.

and then school happened. work happened. the scrolling… happened. you didn't decide to stop playing. you just… stopped.

that's the day you got old. not a birthday. that day.

life felt really short this year. and i caught myself… not playing. so i went back. and found myself present. i re-found myself through playing.

a pool table, a chessboard, a dance floor… i'll play anything. nobody's scouting me. that was never the point. the point is for you to give one evening — to your heart doing that thing again.

i stay present. i notice. i get better.

aware presence is the secret sauce. see clearly, adjust, get back up, go again. let this be the last clip you watch today. go make your own moments. tell your own stories.

play… everything.
