the quantum standard

label the claim.

"Quantum" is the most abused word in spiritual content. AwareWolf earns the right to use it by labelling every quantum claim with its tier, on screen.

finding

settled

Superposition, entanglement, tunnelling, decoherence, quantum field theory, macroscopic quantum states (Nobel 2025). Say these plainly. No hedge needed beyond "this is physics, not metaphysics."

frontier

real but contested

Quantum effects in biology (photosynthesis strong, brain unknown); microtubules and anaesthesia (Neuroscience of Consciousness, 2025 — argued, not accepted); black-hole information recovery. Say "some physicists argue," name them, and say most colleagues disagree.

interpretation

philosophy

What measurement means; whether the observer matters; QBism, relational QM, many-worlds; consciousness-first metaphysics (Kastrup). Say "this is an interpretation" and never present it as a result.

say

  • "the questions have started to rhyme."
  • "this is physics. the meaning is mine."
  • "twenty people, no control group — so, honestly, we don't know."
  • "some physicists argue X. most don't. it's being tested."
  • "the Gītā's word for the body is the physicist's word for reality. that's a coincidence worth sitting with."

never

  • "quantum physics proves…" / "science has confirmed the Vedas."
  • "the observer creates reality" stated as fact.
  • "vibration", "frequency", "manifest", "energy" as physics words.
  • "ancient wisdom knew" without the verse.
  • Any claim without a journal and a year on screen.

the rule beneath the rules

limitation before takeaway.

The tier is not fine print. It is the sentence's first fact. If the sample is small, the result is contested, or the meaning is mine, i say that before i tell you why it matters.
