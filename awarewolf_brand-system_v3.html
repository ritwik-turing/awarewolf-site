02

the name, read through the texts

The name was chosen as a pun. It turns out to be a citation.

वृकvṝka · the wolf

vṝka — the wolf

Sanskrit for wolf, attested in the Ṛgveda. The wolf is the one animal that plays its whole life — not just as a pup. Pack, instinct, night, howl. A werewolf changes when the moon comes out. An aware wolf is the one that noticed it changing.

मृगव्याधmṛgavyādha · the hunter star

mṛgavyādha — the hunter star

A name of Rudra-Śiva in the Sahasranāma: literally "the deer-hunter." In Vedic astronomy it is Sirius — the brightest star in the sky, which the Greeks later called the Dog Star. Your wolf is a name of Śiva that points at a star.

श्वानsvāna · the dog who stayed

svāna — the dog who stayed

In the Mahābhārata's final book, a dog follows Yudhiṣṭhira up the mountain when everyone else has fallen. He refuses heaven without it. The dog is Dharma. Sirius again. This is the brand's word for its followers: the pack — the ones who stay.

चन्द्रमौलिchandramauli · moon-crowned

chandramauli — moon-crowned

Śiva wears the crescent moon: time in phases, the mind that waxes and wanes, cool light after fire. The final emblem does not use the moon as a frame. It carries the same idea of phase through fire, water and the quantum field — transition without disappearance.

The lesson for the brand: never invent symbolism. Find it. Every visual or verbal move should be traceable either to a text with a chapter, or to a paper with a year.

03

cosmology: two gods, one wolf

Sanatan gives the brand two modes, and the quantum field gives them one meeting place. The brand is spiritually open and scientifically agnostic about metaphysical mechanisms: reverent with symbols, exact with claims.

nārāyaṇa mode · the witness

sākṣī · kṣetrajña · yogaḥ

Nārāyaṇa sustains. He is the witness (Sākṣī), the knower of the field (Kṣetrajña), the joining (Yogaḥ). This is the brand's lab and mat voice: steady, precise, citing the journal and the verse, saying the limitation before the claim. Order. Attention. Receipts.

śiva mode · the dancer

naṭarāja · paśupati · mṛgavyādha

Śiva changes form. He is the dancer, the lord of animals, the hunter-star. This is the brand's field voice: the run, the return, the howl, the next pickleball point. Wildness. Play. Practice. The Śiva mode is what keeps the Nārāyaṇa mode from becoming a lecture.

AwareWolf lives at the meeting point: the witness who dances. Every piece of content should have both — one true thing and one live thing. A study with no sweat is a lecture. Sweat with no study is a highlight reel.brand thesis

04

the nine names

Nine names from the two Sahasranāmas, each paired with a real finding from physics or neuroscience and turned into a rule the brand can be held to. The pairings are rhymes, not proofs — and the brand says so, every time. The tier tag is the honesty label: finding settled science · frontier real but contested · interpretation philosophy, not data.

साक्षीsākṣī · the witness
Sākṣī
viṣṇu sahasranāma · 15

the name

The witness. Śaṅkara: the one who sees everything directly, as it is. Not the doer — the one who watches the doing.

the rhyme

interpretation

Quantum theory has no "view from nowhere." In QBism and relational interpretations, a measurement outcome is always an outcome for someone. Physics cannot get rid of the witness; it can only argue about what the witness is.

Rule: first person, always. We say what we saw and did. Never "studies show" without "and here is what happened when I tried it."
क्षेत्रज्ञkṣetrajña · the knower of the field
Kṣetrajña
viṣṇu sahasranāma · 16 · gītā 13.1–2

the name

The knower of the field. The Gītā's thirteenth chapter: the body is the field (kṣetra); that which knows it is the knower. The field is not you. You are what watches the field.

the rhyme

finding

In quantum field theory particles are not things; they are excitations of fields that fill all of space. The field is primary, the particle is an event in it. The Gītā's word for the body is the physicist's word for reality.

Rule: the "field" pillar is literal. Sport, court, pitch, ground. Every week the brand goes into a field and does something with its body, and that is the receipt for everything said about mind.
विश्वम्viśvam · the universe itself
Viśvam
viṣṇu sahasranāma · 1

the name

The universe itself. The first name. Not the maker of the whole — the whole. Everything that exists, as one.

the rhyme

finding

Entanglement (Nobel 2022): two particles can share a single state such that neither has properties on its own — the relation is more fundamental than the parts. Physics has an experimental result that says "separateness is not the deepest layer."

Rule: never mock either side. The scientist and the sādhu are both serious people. Followers are "the pack," never "audience."
अक्षरम्akṣaram · the imperishable
Akṣaram
viṣṇu sahasranāma · 17

the name

The imperishable. That which does not decay however much it is drawn on. Also the word for a syllable — the smallest indestructible unit of speech.

the rhyme

frontier

Quantum information is conserved — unitarity says information is never destroyed, only scrambled. This is why the black-hole information paradox is a paradox, and why the 2019–2025 "island" calculations that recover information from evaporating black holes were treated as a breakthrough. Still frontier.

Rule: receipts are imperishable. Every claim keeps its source; every reel keeps its footage dates; nothing is said that can't be found again.
योगःyogaḥ · the joining
Yogaḥ
viṣṇu sahasranāma · 18

the name

The joining. From yuj, to yoke. Śaṅkara reads it as the means by which the many are brought to the one. Not a pose — a union.

the rhyme

finding

Coherence: many particles acting as a single quantum object. The 2025 Nobel Prize in Physics (Clarke, Devoret, Martinis) honoured the 1985 proof that an entire electrical circuit — billions of electrons — can tunnel as one. Quantum is not only tiny. It is what happens when the many move as one.

Rule: cadence is a practice. One field post a week, one lab post a fortnight, one evening whenever it happens. Same day, same time, the way a practice is kept.
आनन्दःānandaḥ · joy
Ānandaḥ · Nandanaḥ · Nandaḥ
viṣṇu sahasranāma · ~525–527

the name

Bliss; the giver of joy; the one full of joyful things. Three names in a row, as if once was not enough. Joy is not a reward in this tradition. It is a description of the ground.

the rhyme

finding

A 2025 randomised trial (Scientific Reports) found a structured awe practice reduced stress and depressive symptoms and raised well-being. Awe — the feeling of something vast that makes you small — is the one emotion that reliably shrinks the self-focused network in the brain. Play produces it for free.

Rule: show the practice. The return is the receipt that this is joy and not performance. No reel ships without one honest moment of learning.
पशुपतिpaśupati · lord of animals
Paśupati
śiva sahasranāma · anuśāsana parva 17

the name

Lord of animals; lord of all bound beings. Śiva as the one who sits among creatures rather than above them. Also the oldest name in the list — a Paśupati seal appears in the Indus Valley.

the rhyme

frontier

Quantum biology: photosynthetic complexes appear to use quantum coherence to move energy with near-perfect efficiency (RSC review, 2026); birds may navigate by a spin-chemistry compass. Living matter uses quantum effects. Whether the brain does is the open question the new diamond-sensor tools (2026) are built to answer.

Rule: body first. The brand never talks about awareness from a chair for more than one post in a row. The next one is outdoors, sweating.
मृगव्याधmṛgavyādha · the hunter-star
Mṛgavyādha
śiva sahasranāma · sirius

the name

The hunter-star. Rudra as the arrow aimed at the deer (Orion). One target, held steady, in the dark. The brightest star in the sky is a name for aim.

the rhyme

interpretation

Attention is the one thing every consciousness theory agrees matters and no one can define. The 2025 COGITATE experiment (Nature; 256 participants, seven years, $20M) tested the two leading theories against each other and found both wanting — conscious content lived in the back of the brain, not the frontal "CEO." The hunt is still on.

Rule: one target per post. One keyword (PLAY or AWAKE), one study, one practice, one sport. Never two asks in a caption.
चन्द्रमौलिchandramauli · moon-crowned
Chandramauli
śiva sahasranāma · anuśāsana parva 17

the name

Moon-crowned. Śiva wears the waxing crescent — time that returns, the mind that has phases, the cool light that comes after the fire of the third eye.

the rhyme

finding

Decoherence: quantum possibility becomes classical fact by phases — the wave-like state leaks into its surroundings and settles. Nothing collapses all at once; it dephases. The moon is a good model for how a mind clears: not a switch, a cycle.

Rule: phase is a rhythm, not a logo frame. The field-knot is the working motif: two elemental paths meeting through one quantum seam. Content moves in phases: hook → truth → practice → play → howl.

05

the quantum standard

"Quantum" is the most abused word in spiritual content. AwareWolf earns the right to use it by labelling every quantum claim with its tier, on screen.

finding

settled

Superposition, entanglement, tunnelling, decoherence, quantum field theory, macroscopic quantum states (Nobel 2025). Say these plainly. No hedge needed beyond "this is physics, not metaphysics."

frontier

real but contested

Quantum effects in biology (photosynthesis strong, brain unknown); microtubules and anaesthesia (Neuroscience of Consciousness, 2025 — argued, not accepted); black-hole information recovery. Say "some physicists argue," name them, and say most colleagues disagree.

interpretation

philosophy

What measurement means; whether the observer matters; QBism, relational QM, many-worlds; consciousness-first metaphysics (Kastrup). Say "this is an interpretation" and never present it as a result.

say

  • "the questions have started to rhyme."
  • "this is physics. the meaning is mine."
  • "twenty people, no control group — so, honestly, we don't know."
  • "some physicists argue X. most don't. it's being tested."
  • "the Gītā's word for the body is the physicist's word for reality. that's a coincidence worth sitting with."

never

  • "quantum physics proves…" / "science has confirmed the Vedas."
  • "the observer creates reality" stated as fact.
  • "vibration", "frequency", "manifest", "energy" as physics words.
  • "ancient wisdom knew" without the verse.
  • Any claim without a journal and a year on screen.

06

voice & language

First person, lowercase, present tense. Curious, not certain. Confident without pretending mastery. You are the friend who read the paper, sat on the mat, played the point, and stayed present long enough to get better — in that order, every week.

the sentence shapes

  • Limitation → takeaway. "small study. no control group. but still — a week of sitting did what a drug does."
  • Edge → next attempt. "some of this comes easily. some makes me earn it. here's what changed when i stayed with the point."
  • Name → verse → what happened. "kṣetrajña — knower of the field. gītā 13. so i went to an actual field."
  • Question, then silence. "can i tell you something? …" Never answer your own hook in the same breath.
  • Ellipses over commas. The pause is the punctuation.

sanskrit rules

  • Devanāgarī on screen only with an English line directly beneath it.
  • Transliteration uses IAST (kṣetrajña, not kshetragna) in text; captions may drop diacritics for legibility.
  • Every scriptural line carries its address: text · chapter · verse. Sahasranāma names carry the name number where the recension is stable (Viṣṇu), and the source text where it isn't (Śiva).
  • Never translate a name loosely to fit a point. If the meaning is disputed, say "one reading is."
  • Sacred forms are not decoration. Outside the complete master emblem, a trishul, lotus, gadā, Ik Onkar or Dharmachakra appears only when the content is actually about it.

video, motion, sound

picture

  • Real footage only. No stock or generated b-roll. Outside the complete identity, no generic lotus, monk, galaxy, glowing brain or floating sacred object.
  • Vertical 1080×1920. Landscape sources use full-frame blurred letterbox — never crop out the action.
  • Native speed. No slow motion, ever.
  • Beats 1.8–4s; money moments 3–4s; cut on the completed action.
  • Burned-in "receipts" from the original footage (GOAL!, location tags) stay. Brand captions move around them.

captions & motion

  • Anton/Impact, white on black@45%, 16px box padding, centred, y = 13% (or below any existing burned text).
  • Normal line 56px; "big line" 88px in panchaloha or ivory, max three per reel.
  • Every card synced to spoken word timestamps. No card outlives its sentence by more than 0.9s.
  • Pop-in 0.4s alpha ramp. No slides, no bounces, no zoom-punches.
  • Evidence lower-third: Karla caps, quantum cyan, "journal · month year · n=" — appears for the full duration of the claim.

sound

  • VO: Ritwik VO on ElevenLabs Multilingual v2 — stability 0.31, similarity 1.0, style 1.0, speed 1.1. No inline tags.
  • VO placed sentence by sentence with 0.3–1.0s breaths; never a gap over 1.5s mid-reel.
  • Bed: the friends' jam, looped, at 0.85 under VO; clip audio at 0.22.
  • Endings are one continuous recording — pre-rolled under the last shots, no splices.
  • Last word ends ~0.4s before the closing cut; the closer's music is already up.

11

the pack

Svāna, the dog who stayed. Followers are not an audience; they are the ones who came along. The community system is built on proof, not applause.

the proof loop

Comment the keyword → get the page → screenshot your pick → go do it → send the proof. Every proof is reposted to stories with the mark stamp. Every month the proofs become a reel: "you actually went."

the howl

Sunday night story: one question, "what did you play this week?" Answers reposted. It is the brand's only recurring ritual and it never sells anything.

pack rules

Reply to every comment in the first hour in your texting voice. Never pin praise. Pin the clearest learning moment. Correct yourself publicly when a study turns out weaker than you said — that correction is content.

  • No banned words (manifest, vibration, frequency, "quantum" untiered).
