the name

read through the texts.

The name was chosen as a pun. It turns out to be a citation.

वृकvṝka — the wolf

vṝka — the wolf

Sanskrit for wolf, attested in the Ṛgveda. The wolf is the one animal that plays its whole life — not just as a pup. Pack, instinct, night, howl. A werewolf changes when the moon comes out. An aware wolf is the one that noticed it changing.

मृगव्याधmṛgavyādha — the hunter star

mṛgavyādha — the hunter star

A name of Rudra-Śiva in the Sahasranāma: literally "the deer-hunter." In Vedic astronomy it is Sirius — the brightest star in the sky, which the Greeks later called the Dog Star. Your wolf is a name of Śiva that points at a star.

श्वानsvāna — the dog who stayed

svāna — the dog who stayed

In the Mahābhārata's final book, a dog follows Yudhiṣṭhira up the mountain when everyone else has fallen. He refuses heaven without it. The dog is Dharma. Sirius again. This is the brand's word for its followers: the pack — the ones who stay.

चन्द्रमौलिchandramauli — moon-crowned

chandramauli — moon-crowned

Śiva wears the crescent moon. Your logo's wolf sits inside a crescent. The crescent is time in phases, the mind that waxes and wanes, and the thing the wolf howls at. It is also a motion line — the wolf is moving fast. All three readings are correct and the brand uses all three.

The lesson for the brand: never invent symbolism. Find it. Every visual or verbal move should be traceable either to a text with a chapter, or to a paper with a year.
