the nine names

rhymes, not proofs.

Nine names from the two Sahasranāmas, each paired with a real finding from physics or neuroscience and turned into a rule the brand can be held to. The pairings are rhymes, not proofs — and the brand says so, every time.

साक्षीSākṣī · the witness

Sākṣī

viṣṇu sahasranāma · 15

the name

The witness. Śaṅkara: the one who sees everything directly, as it is. Not the doer — the one who watches the doing.

interpretation

Quantum theory has no "view from nowhere." In QBism and relational interpretations, a measurement outcome is always an outcome for someone. Physics cannot get rid of the witness; it can only argue about what the witness is.

Rule: first person, always. We say what we saw and did. Never "studies show" without "and here is what happened when I tried it."

क्षेत्रज्ञKṣetrajña · the knower of the field

Kṣetrajña

viṣṇu sahasranāma · 16 · gītā 13.1–2

the name

The knower of the field. The Gītā's thirteenth chapter: the body is the field (kṣetra); that which knows it is the knower. The field is not you. You are what watches the field.

finding

In quantum field theory particles are not things; they are excitations of fields that fill all of space. The field is primary, the particle is an event in it. The Gītā's word for the body is the physicist's word for reality.

Rule: the "field" pillar is literal. Sport, court, pitch, ground. Every week the brand goes into a field and does something with its body, and that is the receipt for everything said about mind.

विश्वम्Viśvam · the universe itself

Viśvam

viṣṇu sahasranāma · 1

the name

The universe itself. The first name. Not the maker of the whole — the whole. Everything that exists, as one.

finding

Entanglement (Nobel 2022): two particles can share a single state such that neither has properties on its own — the relation is more fundamental than the parts. Physics has an experimental result that says "separateness is not the deepest layer."

Rule: never mock either side. The scientist and the sādhu are both serious people. Followers are "the pack," never "audience."

अक्षरम्Akṣaram · the imperishable

Akṣaram

viṣṇu sahasranāma · 17

the name

The imperishable. That which does not decay however much it is drawn on. Also the word for a syllable — the smallest indestructible unit of speech.

frontier

Quantum information is conserved — unitarity says information is never destroyed, only scrambled. This is why the black-hole information paradox is a paradox, and why the 2019–2025 "island" calculations that recover information from evaporating black holes were treated as a breakthrough. Still frontier.

Rule: receipts are imperishable. Every claim keeps its source; every reel keeps its footage dates; nothing is said that can't be found again.

योगःYogaḥ · the joining

Yogaḥ

viṣṇu sahasranāma · 18

the name

The joining. From yuj, to yoke. Śaṅkara reads it as the means by which the many are brought to the one. Not a pose — a union.

finding

Coherence: many particles acting as a single quantum object. The 2025 Nobel Prize in Physics (Clarke, Devoret, Martinis) honoured the 1985 proof that an entire electrical circuit — billions of electrons — can tunnel as one. Quantum is not only tiny. It is what happens when the many move as one.

Rule: cadence is a practice. One field post a week, one lab post a fortnight, one evening whenever it happens. Same day, same time, the way a practice is kept.

आनन्दःĀnandaḥ · joy

Ānandaḥ · Nandanaḥ · Nandaḥ

viṣṇu sahasranāma · ~525–527

the name

Bliss; the giver of joy; the one full of joyful things. Three names in a row, as if once was not enough. Joy is not a reward in this tradition. It is a description of the ground.

finding

A 2025 randomised trial (Scientific Reports) found a structured awe practice reduced stress and depressive symptoms and raised well-being. Awe — the feeling of something vast that makes you small — is the one emotion that reliably shrinks the self-focused network in the brain. Play produces it for free.

Rule: show the practice. The return is the receipt that this is joy and not performance. No reel ships without one honest moment of learning.

पशुपतिPaśupati · lord of animals

Paśupati

śiva sahasranāma · anuśāsana parva 17

the name

Lord of animals; lord of all bound beings. Śiva as the one who sits among creatures rather than above them. Also the oldest name in the list — a Paśupati seal appears in the Indus Valley.

frontier

Quantum biology: photosynthetic complexes appear to use quantum coherence to move energy with near-perfect efficiency (RSC review, 2026); birds may navigate by a spin-chemistry compass. Living matter uses quantum effects. Whether the brain does is the open question the new diamond-sensor tools (2026) are built to answer.

Rule: body first. The brand never talks about awareness from a chair for more than one post in a row. The next one is outdoors, sweating.

मृगव्याधMṛgavyādha · the hunter-star

Mṛgavyādha

śiva sahasranāma · sirius

the name

The hunter-star. Rudra as the arrow aimed at the deer (Orion). One target, held steady, in the dark. The brightest star in the sky is a name for aim.

interpretation

Attention is the one thing every consciousness theory agrees matters and no one can define. The 2025 COGITATE experiment (Nature; 256 participants, seven years, $20M) tested the two leading theories against each other and found both wanting — conscious content lived in the back of the brain, not the frontal "CEO." The hunt is still on.

Rule: one target per post. One keyword (PLAY or AWAKE), one study, one practice, one sport. Never two asks in a caption.

चन्द्रमौलिChandramauli · moon-crowned

Chandramauli

śiva sahasranāma · the logo's crescent

the name

Moon-crowned. Śiva wears the waxing crescent — time that returns, the mind that has phases, the cool light that comes after the fire of the third eye.

finding

Decoherence: quantum possibility becomes classical fact by phases — the wave-like state leaks into its surroundings and settles. Nothing collapses all at once; it dephases. The moon is a good model for how a mind clears: not a switch, a cycle.

Rule: the crescent is the brand's only motif and its time signature. Angel-number durations (22.22, 33.33 … 99.99s) on every reel. Content moves in phases: hook → truth → practice → play → howl.
